About

Lead with an impactful statement.

My name is Lejla, and my expertise lies in improving the employee experience by aligning people, technology, and systems, both strategically and operationally. I design action plans that drive incremental change, and I have strong experience in automating HR processes. The core of my work is creating a people first culture while keeping the business in mind. I help with change management by bringing people on board and successfully leading change.

My Background

Over my 15+ years of working in different industries (Banking, Finance, Telecommunications, Airline, IT, deep-tech startups), I've had the privilege of working on impactful projects that were protagonists from the start, driven by employee data analysis where I was able to implement new technology solutions.

My background is in Financial Management, where I started working on analyzing financial ratios for SMEs. However, I accidentally began working on People Metrics and Analytics.

Later, I developed into a role where I advise leaders on ad hoc action plans when it comes to HR policy and Compliance, working on streamlining People processes and procedures globally. I worked on AI integration in HR in one of the best IT companies.

Those experiences taught me the importance of having the right tools/systems, as well as the importance of aligning people, processes, and technologies — all to enable people and businesses to be efficient and productive.

I created this website with a focus on helping you, as a business, to improve those elements above in order to ensure that your business is efficient and, most importantly, alive.

Projects

AI-HR integration project

Centralized HR tools, simplified the intranet, and improved AI search capabilities. I collaborated with HR stakeholders to integrate the HR AI bot, while ensuring intranet data stayed up to date and accurate.

Owned ticket-system optimization and change management by simplifying ServiceNow workflows, extracting and analyzing ticket data, selecting automation opportunities, and partnering closely with IT and People Tech teams to implement improvements. Employee feedback was incorporated into action plans throughout the project.

In parallel, I drafted and updated EMEA HR policies across 22 countries.

Expected benefits: 24/7 support for common HR questions, a unified employee experience, and manager self-service HR transactions instead of manual HR handoffs.

How we measured impact:

  • 30% ticket reduction target
  • Time to find HR information reduced from 10 minutes to 2 minutes
  • Time to receive answers reduced from 3 days to 1 day
  • Tier-1 bot enablement target to reduce tickets by up to 85% through self-service

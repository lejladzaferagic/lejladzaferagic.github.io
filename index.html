HR Chatbot integration presentation

Leveraging HR chatbot capabilities to improve employee experience across multiple HR systems, scale support, and provide faster response times.

Results and how we measure it: Reduced employee tickets by 57% in 3 months (target 30%); centralized HR tools; simplified intranet; improved AI search. The expected benefits included providing 24/7 support for common HR questions, creating a unified employee experience, and enabling manager self-service for HR transactions, while success was measured by a 30 percent reduction in tickets, reducing information search time from 10 minutes to 2 minutes, shortening response time from 3 days to 1 day, and enabling up to 85 percent ticket reduction through Tier 1 bot self-service.
